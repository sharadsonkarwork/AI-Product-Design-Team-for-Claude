## Long description
Designing a whole product before anyone checks the scope is expensive. Product Design Pipeline makes Claude work the way strong product teams do: define the problem, scope the smallest valuable release, test a cheap concept, and only then invest in detailed design.

An orchestrator leads 14 specialist agents through four phases:
- **Define:** research, requirements, effort estimates, and an MVP scope scored with MoSCoW and value vs effort, with testable hypotheses and go / no-go criteria.
- **Concept:** personas (including people with disabilities), journeys, draft copy, a clickable concept prototype, and persona walkthroughs.
- **Build out the MVP:**
  - technical design;
  - semantic design tokens and accessible components;
  - final copy with an audit that removes AI-sounding text;
  - a high-fidelity prototype;
  - design critique and full WCAG 2.2 AA validation;
  - the developer handoff and an analytics plan.
- **Case study:** a visual portfolio case study (web page, plus Figma frames when connected). It covers the problem, research, personas, jobs to be done, journey map and MVP decisions. Badges link each design decision to the Law of UX behind it. An anonymised version is available for NDA work.
- **Check and plan:** QA of the built product, a usability test plan, and a go / iterate / pivot decision based on real results.

The pipeline stops for your approval at each checkpoint. You can start at any phase with the work you already have, or call a single specialist on its own. Prototypes are built in Figma when it's connected, otherwise as accessible HTML.
